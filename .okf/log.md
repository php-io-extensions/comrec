# Log

## 2026-11-13

* Bundle created with ext-pixel-queue 0.11.0: [queue](api/queue.md) (`PixelQueue`, `PixelQueueException`, 13 constants), [build](runbooks/build.md). A frame of commands under a transform and a clip, nine shape methods, `append()` for commands made elsewhere, layers, and `drawn()` / `changed()` over the last two frames. `PHP_PIXEL_QUEUE_VERSION` 0.11.0, `branch-alias` `dev-main` is `0.11.x-dev`, on the Venusian 0.11 line. `config.m4` passes `-ffp-contract=off -fno-builtin-sin -fno-builtin-cos`: the first build without the last two called `__sincos_stret` and gave a flattened ellipse's points a different last bit from separate `sin()` and `cos()`. Built with `-Wall -Wextra` with no warning from `src/`, `fmadd` count 0, installed and 115 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64), NTS also under guard malloc. Not yet built on Linux.

## 2026-11-18

* `PixelQueue::fillPath()` and `strokePath()`: paths of any number of contours, and stroked lines through any number of points, recorded under the transform and the clip as the shape methods record theirs (see [queue](api/queue.md)), so a caller never transforms points itself. 18 hand-worked tests in `tests/PathsTest.php`; each of three deliberate breaks (the rule value ignored, the transform skipped, a one-point stroke recorded) fails one. 146 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64); `fmadd` count 0. Topside's PHP recorder gives the same commands, `changed()` rects and refusals over 900 random scenes. Not yet built on Linux.
* `fillPath()` and `strokePath()` take path ops (`M`, `L`, `Q`, `C`, `Z`) in place of point lists and flatten curves after the transform, by the steps in [queue](api/queue.md#flattening-a-curve). 23 tests in `tests/PathsTest.php`; four deliberate breaks (a quadratic's piece count, a cubic's sum, no new subpath after `Z`, pieces counted before the transform) each fail. 151 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64); `fmadd` 0, no `sincos`. Topside's PHP recorder gives the same commands, `changed()` rects and refusals over 900 random scenes of lines, quadratics and cubics. Not yet built on Linux.
* Paints: every shape method that took an `rgba` takes an int colour or a gradient `[kind, geometry, stops, spread]`, recorded with the transform appended (see [queue](api/queue.md#paints)). 6 tests in `tests/PaintTest.php`; three deliberate breaks (no transform appended, a list of three let through, the transform taken as identity) each fail. 157 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64). Topside's PHP recorder gives the same commands over 900 random scenes with gradients. Not yet built on Linux.

## 2026-11-19

* `PixelQueue::lower(command, form)`: a command as `'polygon'`, `'trapezoids'` or `'triangles'` for a drawer whose library cannot draw it as it is (see [queue](api/queue.md#lowering)); the steps are `src/lower.c`, plain C. Topside's PHP `Lowering` gives identical results on 3,618 lowerings of random paths (self-crossing, both rules), ellipses, rings, rounded boxes and rings, and polylines with repeats and reversals. Against inside tests at sample points, the trapezoids and triangles cover what the polygon's rule covers at every one of 1.86 million points. A stroke was first lowered as a quad a side and a piece a corner: a 1,024-sided ring became about 2,000 overlapping contours, every crossing cut a band across the shape, and one random ring ran PHP past 1 GB. It is now one outline a side (Skia's construction, inner corners through the corner's point), which covers the same points (0 of 1.4 million differ) with about a quarter of the trapezoids. 8 tests in `tests/LowerTest.php`, worked by hand; each fails when its part is broken (even-odd read as non-zero, no cut at a crossing, the inner corner's point dropped, the miter limit ignored). Built with `-Wall -Wextra` with no warning from `src/`, `fmadd` 0 and no `sincos` in `lower.o`; installed and 165 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64). Not yet built on Linux.
* `lower()`: a trapezoid stays open while the same two edges bound it, so one shape is no longer cut at the corners of another in the same path. The particle field in topside's demo (132 contours, 1,552 points) lowered to 15,410 trapezoids and is now 334. Band edges are insertion-sorted from the last band's order, and under non-zero the crossings inside a sealed group are not cut (see [queue](api/queue.md#lowering)): on that path the band calls went from 7,099 to 955, and a lowering from 17.28 ms to 1.23 ms. A `'trapezoids'` command lowers on to `'triangles'`. Topside's PHP `Lowering` gives identical results on 10,836 random lowerings, 60 particle fields and their chained triangles; the trapezoids cover what the rule covers at every sample point on 13 seeds, the dense ones with 20-point self-crossing paths. An earlier rule that skipped a crossing by the windings around two edges alone was unsound (three interior edges reordering can open a gap) and failed that check at 16 points; it was replaced by the sealed groups. 2 more tests in `tests/LowerTest.php` and the nested-squares test worked again by hand; three deliberate breaks fail (carrying off, a sort reversed, every group sealed). 167 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64); `fmadd` 0, no `sincos`. Not yet built on Linux.
* `lower()` takes a third argument, `packing`: a lowered form's items as packed strings, laid out as the drawer asks (`'f32'` or `'fixed16'`, fields in a given order), so a library's struct array is built natively, not in PHP (see [queue](api/queue.md#lowering)). Topside's PHP `Lowering` gives identical strings on 5,064 packed lowerings over three seeds, and the strings decode to the list form. 2 tests in `tests/LowerTest.php`, worked by hand; two deliberate breaks (fixed16 truncated, fields ignored) each fail. On topside's demo at 1280×840, the particle field on pixman went from 13.45 ms a frame to 6.21. 169 passed on Homebrew PHP 8.4.27 NTS and ZTS (macOS arm64). Not yet built on Linux.
